## Smart Keyboard 1.3.0 — offline AI, Arabizi, voice typing, sync and lots more looks

**Install:** download `SmartKeyboard-v1.3.0.apk` below, open it on your phone, then open the
Smart Keyboard app. Android 8.0+.

### Offline AI — no key, no internet
- **AI assistant → Offline AI**: download a model once and everything AI runs on your phone. **Gemma 4 E2B** (Google, best in English, German, Arabic and 140+ languages, 2 GB — great on the Galaxy S24), **Qwen 3 1.7B** (1 GB) or **Qwen 3 0.6B** (345 MB). Downloads keep going in the background and are checked before use.
- **Gemini Nano** is used where Android has it built in.
- With an online provider, the offline model can take over automatically when there's no internet.

### Arabizi
- On the English keyboard (with Arabic enabled): `7abibi → حبيبي`, `3arabi → عربي`, `2albi → قلبي`, `kifak → كيفك`. Words with 2/3/5/6/7/8/9 convert on space; right after Arabic, plain Latin Arabizi does too; otherwise you get an Arabic suggestion. Backspace undoes it, and it learns your spellings. Prefers everyday dialect words.

### Voice typing
- 🎤 on the suggestion bar. Uses the phone's offline speech recogniser when it has one; what you say is proofread the chill way (in the app's tone) and can be undone with one tap.

### Tone per app
- Chill in WhatsApp/Snapchat/Instagram, clean in Slack/Teams, polite in Gmail/Outlook — automatically. Change any app under AI assistant → Tone per app.

### Your own shortcuts
- Add your own ("omw" → "On my way!"): offered as a suggestion, or expanded on space if you turn that on. Nothing built in.

### Sync between phones
- Everything learned, end-to-end encrypted with your passphrase (AES-256), in one file in Google Drive or any folder. Pick the same file on your other phone. Syncs in the background.

### More looks
- 14 new themes (Midnight, Nord, Dracula, Solarized, Mint, Lavender, Aurora, Rose Gold, Cyberpunk, Paper, Slate, Berry, Carbon, Sky) and **your own colours** with any accent colour.
- Key fonts, capital letters on keys, key shadows on/off, language name on the space bar, swipe trail colour (accent, rainbow, letters) and thickness.

### Also
- Swipe: a word that goes back and forth between neighbouring keys ("there") is recognised.
- A word you brought back with backspace stays exactly as typed.
- Backup import now merges with what's on the phone instead of overwriting it.
