## Smart Keyboard 1.3.1 — no more waiting for offline AI, better emoji

**Install:** download `SmartKeyboard-v1.3.1.apk` below, open it on your phone, then open the
Smart Keyboard app. Android 8.0+.

### Fixed
- **The keyboard no longer freezes while the offline AI loads.** Opening the keyboard in a chat could make it wait ~10 seconds for the offline model; the model now loads quietly in the background at low priority, and typing never waits for it. It also stays loaded for 20 minutes after you last used it (was 5), so it reloads much less often.
- **Picking an emoji suggestion replaces the word**: "sob" → 😭, not "sob 😭".
- **Swiped words get emoji suggestions too.**

### Better emoji
- Many more chat words: "sob" 😭, "dead" 💀, "slay" 💅, "smh" 🤦, "shrug" 🤷, "pls" 🥺, "yay" 🥳 and lots more, in English, German and Arabic.
- Laughter in any spelling ("hahaha", "looool", "lmaooo", "xD", "jajaja", "هههه") and stretched words ("sooob", "loooove").
- **Recently used** is always the first section of the emoji panel and now includes emoji you picked from the suggestion bar. Until you've used any, popular ones fill it.
